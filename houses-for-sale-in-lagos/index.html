Checks to make before you pay for a house in Lagos

  1. See the title document. Usually a Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) or Governor's Consent. If the land was once community land, ask for the gazette or excision too.
  2. Have the title searched. An independent lawyer can search it at the Lagos State Lands Registry to confirm who owns it and whether there is any dispute or charge on it.
  3. Use your own lawyer and surveyor. Not the seller's. The surveyor confirms the plot matches the survey plan and is not on government-acquired land.
  4. Inspect, in person or by live video. Visit at different times of day if you can, and ask the neighbours about flooding and the road in the rainy season.
  5. Pay into a verified account. Never in cash and never to a middleman. Get a receipt and a signed deed of assignment.
  6. Perfect the title after you buy. Register the deed and apply for Governor's Consent so the house is legally in your name.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Always use a qualified Lagos property lawyer.

Questions people ask

How much is a house in Lagos?

In October 2026 the median asking price was ₦353.1 million. Half of all houses asked between ₦189.6 million and ₦672 million. Size and area change this a lot: a 3-bedroom house had a median of ₦156 million, and Ikoyi's median was over ₦2.3 billion.

Where are the cheapest houses in Lagos?

On the edges of the state. Badagry (about ₦29 million, from few listings), Ikorodu (₦53.1 million), Epe (₦57.9 million), Ibeju-Lekki (₦93.9 million) and Ayobo (₦94.9 million) had the lowest medians in October 2026.

What documents should a house in Lagos have?

A title document, usually a C of O or Governor's Consent, plus the survey plan and the deed of assignment from the seller. Have your own lawyer search the title at the Lagos State Lands Registry before you pay.

Can I buy a house in Lagos from abroad?

Yes. Many buyers inspect by video call, hire their own Lagos lawyer to check the title, and pay by bank transfer to a verified account. We can walk you through each step by WhatsApp.

Are these the prices houses actually sell for?

No, they are asking prices from listings. Sale prices are often lower after negotiation, and a house can sell above or below its area's median depending on its condition, title and street.
