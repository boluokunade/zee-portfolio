Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how Zee Realty Ltd (RC 6892262), 70B Olorunlogbon Street, Anthony Village, Lagos, Nigeria ("Zee Realty", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal information when you visit our websites, fill in one of our forms (including forms inside Facebook and Instagram ads), or contact us on WhatsApp, phone or email.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, WhatsApp or phone number, email address, the property you are interested in, when you plan to be in Lagos, your budget and any message you send.
  • How you use our websites: pages visited, buttons tapped (for example "Get started" or "Chat on WhatsApp"), the ad or link that brought you to us, your device and browser type and your approximate location. We collect this with cookies and similar tools from Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads) and Meta (the Meta Pixel).

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the property information you asked for, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To help you buy, view or manage a property with us.
  • To measure how our ads and websites perform and to show our ads to people who are likely to be interested.
  • To keep records required by law and to protect against fraud.

We use your details because you asked us to contact you (your consent), to take steps you asked for before a purchase, and for our legitimate interest in running and improving our business.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business: Google (forms, email, spreadsheets, analytics and ads), Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp) and our email provider. They process your data on our behalf.
  • The developer or property manager of a property you choose to buy, only when you go ahead with that property.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria, for example in the United States or the European Union. They protect it with their own security measures and contractual safeguards.

Cookies and your choices

Our landing page shows a cookie bar. If you are in the UK or Europe, we only use advertising and analytics cookies after you tap "Accept". Everywhere else, you can turn them off at any time by tapping "No thanks" or "Cookie choices" at the bottom of the page. You can also block cookies in your browser settings and control ad personalisation in your Google and Meta account settings.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the UK GDPR and similar laws, you can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Just email hello@zeerealtyhq.com or reply "STOP" on WhatsApp. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or the data protection authority where you live.

Security

We keep your information in password-protected accounts that only our team can access, and we never ask for bank or card details through our forms.
